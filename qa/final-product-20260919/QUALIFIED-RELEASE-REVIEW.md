# Qualified release review — performance and startup

Decision: accept performance and startup-stability as disclosed limitations for a qualified release. This decision does not mark the 2% benchmark passed, repair the startup condition, or substitute for installer correctness. The user requested a Nexus-ready product; the parent authorized this explicit engineering release policy after reviewing the evidence. No user-specified numeric criterion was found or relaxed.

Performance: September 18 TESTING-RECORD explicitly says no performance requirements existed and proposes the 2% target. The normal foreground 60-second sample measured 2.106183% of eight logical processors and 475,410,432 bytes peak combined working set. The latter passes even decimal 500 MB; earlier raw latency results remain within the proposed median/p95 budgets. Native sampled stacks identify substantial accessibility/COM servicing, with caller attribution unresolved. Background and accessibility-disabled results are diagnostic comparisons, not substitutes for foreground data. The small benchmark miss remains public in known limitations; no FPS, zero-overhead or optimization claim is made.

Startup: stalled clean-unmodded3 followed two confirmed unmodded title-screen sessions, with no proxy/UE4SS loaded in the stalled process. Its game/render/RHI wait signatures match prior modded hangs. This establishes that active menu runtime is not necessary for the observed failure; it does not prove a driver/game root cause or eliminate the user's startup risk. Ordinary connected sessions and loaded-save graceful shutdown are verified separately. No late injection, overlay attribution or other unproved remedy is shipped. The page describes the intermittent condition and recommends the verified normal quit path without promising prevention.

Release rationale: neither record presently demonstrates an unfixed menu-specific functional defect or violates a located numeric user requirement. Accurate caveats support a useful qualified release; claiming perfection would not. The concrete native installer byte-restoration defect must pass its own corrected-candidate roundtrip. Feature and lifecycle scopes still require passed evidence; installer-roundtrip may never be qualified by this policy.

Gate policy: only performance and startup-stability may use accepted-with-limitations. Such records require a release-with-disclosed-limitations decision, written rationale, a hash-bound review artifact, and ordinary hash-bound evidence. Missing/tampered review, empty rationale/evidence, unsupported decision, and qualification of features/lifecycle/installer are rejected. The gate source itself is part of required identity bindings. Existing three feature-effect exceptions remain separate and unchanged.

Evidence is bound in the per-scope review JSON files and current acceptance ledger.
